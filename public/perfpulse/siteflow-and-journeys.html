Information architecture

Sitemap / Siteflow

The product has four surfaces, each scoped by who owns the data. The One-CTO prototype screens sit inside the PortCo workspace, under Onboarding. Sales handoff, company-record creation, and launch-onboarding connect the surfaces.

End-to-end

9-screen prototype path

Phase 1 Public Scan (screens A–D) hands directly into Phase 2 One-CTO Onboarding (screens 1–5), then the company workspace becomes the ongoing home.

User journey

Journey 1 · Public Inquiry / Scan

Entry: a visitor reaches the site. Exit: the visitor requests a call, which hands off to Journey 2, or stays in the nurture drip. Decision gates: code validity, scan match (Q8–9), pulse-check opt-in, and call request.

This flow is laid out in stages — scroll sideways if the full width is wider than your window.

User journey

Journey 2 · Direct Sales → One-CTO Onboarding

Entry: sales contacts an inquiry, or the PE Operations Team launches onboarding (Journey 3). Exit: the Baseline Date is recorded and the company workspace becomes the ongoing home. Collection starts only after Sponsor sign-off and Security approval (D28 locked: no separate release step).

User journey

Journey 3 · Private Equity Portfolio Rollout

Entry: PerfPulse creates the PE firm. Exit: each PortCo enters Journey 2. Firm-level Ask stays OFF unless it is explicitly enabled. D44 parent/sibling switch is locked default OFF.
