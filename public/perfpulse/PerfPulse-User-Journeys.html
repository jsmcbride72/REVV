User Journey 1

Public Inquiry & Outside-In Scan

A visitor arrives with no account, verifies a work email with a 6-digit code, reviews their company's detected tools, and either continues into onboarding or books a sales call.

Entry / end state Screen or step Decision point Role handoff Optional / post-demo

1Arrive & Verify Email

Visitor lands on site
↓
Scan offer pageExplains free outside-in scan and privacy notice
↓
Enter work emailNo password required; personal domains blocked
↓
6-digit magic code emailed
↓
Decision Visitor enters valid code?
Valid Proceeds to Stage 2 domain scan.
Invalid / expired Inline error + Resend code (or reminder email if abandoned).

2Scan & Confirm Systems

Identify company & run outside-in scanMatches company by work email domain and detects public tool signals
↓
Decision Company matched cleanly?
Yes Shows detected systems list.
No / ambiguous Visitor confirms company domain and retries scan.
↓
Review & confirm systemsMark each tool as used / not used / unsure, add missed tools, and record system owners. Inferred vs. stated data is saved side by side.
↓
Company record createdPrivacy disclosure confirms results stay private while keeping a company record for consultant follow-up.

3Pulse Check & Next Step

Decision Take optional pulse-check?
Yes Completes short self-assessment questions; feedback captured.
Skip Goes straight to results & next-step options.
↓
Results summary & CTAsContinue to Record Ready, Book a call, or Email results and exit
↓
Decision Continues or books a call?
Yes Handoff → Journey 2 Enters Record Ready / Sales discovery.
Not now Automated email & marketing drip seeded with scan results; later CTA click re-enters Journey 2.
↓
Handoff to Sales / Onboarding (Journey 2)

User Journey 3

Private Equity Portfolio Rollout

How a parent PE firm loads portfolio companies, runs outside-in scans across the portfolio, launches pilots, and controls cross-company sharing.

Entry / end state Screen or step Decision point Role handoff Optional / post-demo

1Set Up Parent & Portfolio

Sales / Parent Lead creates parent & domain records
↓
Link portfolio companiesLoad PortCos (e.g., ACME + Proforma Company)
↓
Run outside-in scans across PortCos
↓
Gated portfolio previewParent prospect verifies email and reviews initial scan before first meeting
↓
Present initial portfolio viewIncludes Proforma Company benchmark + drip email campaign to firm & PortCo stakeholders

2Deal, Pilots & Rollout

Decision Parent signs data-access agreement?
Yes Proceeds to confirm company list and select 1–2 pilot PortCos.
Not yet Continues drip campaign until ready.
↓
Operations Team manages rolloutConfigures per-PortCo access & sharing grids
↓
Invite PortCo rolesExecutive Sponsor, AI Lead, Security Reviewer, System Admin
↓
Each PortCo runs Journey 2 (One-CTO Onboarding)
↓
Assisted onboarding mode (optional)Performance Pulse Onboarding Lead can complete logged steps on a PortCo's behalf (final scope TBD).

3Track, Share & Sibling Access

Portfolio rollout trackerShows live onboarding stage for every PortCo
↓
Board Member accessRead-only views scoped strictly to assigned companies
↓
Default Setting Parent-firm sharing switch (default: OFF)
OFF (default) Sibling companies see outside-in tool counts only — scores off, no source evidence.
ON (opt-in) Siblings can request named analysis, still with zero source evidence shared.
